The cottage

Where the forest meets the open sky

High in the Ukrainian Carpathians, in the village of Yablunytsya, Jazz Xata is a two-storey wooden cottage with two terraces, two bedrooms, two bathrooms, a kitchenette, and a living room built around a stone fireplace — comfortable enough for four to five guests, quiet enough to actually rest.

After a day in the mountains, the sauna is ready. Dinner can be made in the fully equipped kitchen or ordered in. The barbecue and gazebo in the courtyard are perfect for long summer evenings. It's the Carpathians the way they should be experienced — slow, warm, and lived in.

What makes Jazz Xata different

Music, books, and a warm fire

Most rentals give you a place to sleep. Jazz Xata gives you a place to do something. Musical instruments wait to be played, the bookshelves are stocked, and there are sketchbooks and art supplies for anyone who feels inspired.

Guests come for the skiing and hiking, and often stay an extra day to read by the fire or write at the kitchen table.

FAQ

Good to know

How many people does Jazz Xata sleep?
Comfortably 4, with space for a 5th guest.
Is the cottage suitable for winter?
Yes — the fireplace and sauna make it ideal in winter. Plan to arrive in a 4WD vehicle, or use the lower parking and we'll help you up.
Can we order food?
Yes. The kitchen is fully equipped, but dinner can also be ordered in.
Can the host take us to the trails or ski lifts?
Yes — transfers to Hoverla, Petros, Khomyak, Pip Ivan, Bukovel, and Dragobrat can be arranged.
Is there Wi-Fi?
Yes, throughout the cottage.
